# News Article Page

## Overview
A newspaper-style layout designed with classic editorial typography and semantic text formatting.

## What I Practiced
- Text formatting elements: `<mark>` (highlights), `<del>` / `<ins>` (editorial edits), and `<sub>` / `<sup>` (scientific formulas).
- Newspaper-style header with publication date and category tags.
- Serif typography paired with clean line-height for readability.

## Files
- `news-article.html`: Article markup and formatting.
- `style.css`: Editorial typography and callout box styles.

## How to View
Open `news-article.html` in your browser.
