Why Semantic HTML Matters

Published on July 31, 2026 by Muqadas Akram

Semantic HTML introduces tags that clearly describe their meaning to both the browser and the developer. Key examples include: <header>, <article>, <section>, and <footer>.

These elements make websites easier to understand for developers, browsers, search engine web crawlers, and screen readers.
