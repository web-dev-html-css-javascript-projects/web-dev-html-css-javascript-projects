# E-Commerce Product Card

## Overview
A product card component commonly used in online stores, designed with a side-by-side image and details layout.

## What I Practiced
- Split card design using flexbox (image on the left, details on the right).
- Sale badge positioning and price discount styling with `<del>`.
- Hover transitions on action buttons ("Add to Cart" and "Buy Now").
- Responsive behavior (stacks image on top for mobile screens).

## Files
- `product-card.html`: Product card markup.
- `style.css`: Card layout, buttons, and responsive styles.

## How to View
Open `product-card.html` in your browser.
